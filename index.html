📖 How to Use Punchcard Scanner

📥

1. Import Photo

Drag & drop or click to upload a photo of your physical punchcard or chart.

📏

2. Set Grid Size

Enter the exact columns (stitches) and rows (height) of your physical punchcard (e.g., 24 columns for standard Brother punchcards).

📍

3. Align Corner Pins

The app will attempt to automatically find the grid corners on upload. You can re-trigger this with the Auto-Detect button, or manually drag the handles (TL, TR, BL, BR) for pixel-perfect alignment.

⚙️

4. Image Tuning

Adjust the Scan Threshold (darkness cutoff) and Sampling Width (pixel averaging size) until the preview correctly detects your card's holes.

✏️

5. Manual Corrections (Optional)

Click any cell in the preview grid to toggle its value, or click-and-drag to paint/erase multiple cells. This activates Edit Mode, locking calibration handles and settings to safeguard your edits. Click Reset to Scan to discard your manual edits and unlock controls.

💾

6. Export Grid

Download the scanned PNG. The exported image will be exactly the specified dimensions in pixels (e.g. 24x60), ready for AYAB mapping!

💡 Tips for Best Auto-Detection

  • Contrasting Background: Place your light-colored punchcard on a dark table or surface.
  • Good Lighting: Avoid heavy shadows or bright glares over the card's corners.
  • Full View: Ensure all four corners are completely visible inside the photo frame.
