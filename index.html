📖 How to Use Punchcard Scanner

📥

1. Import Photo

Drag & drop or click to upload a photo of your physical punchcard or chart.

🎨

2. Choose Pattern Type

Select how the pattern marks are represented:
• 🕳️ Punchcard: Circular punched holes in card cells (uses central radial sampling).
• ❌ Cross Stitch: Crosses ('X'), plusses ('+'), or stitch symbols inside cells (uses stroke/cross analysis and margin inset to ignore chart gridlines).
• ⬛ Crochet Filet: Solid filled squares vs empty mesh (evaluates overall cell fill coverage).

📏

3. Set Grid Size

Enter the exact columns (stitches) and rows (height) of your physical chart or punchcard.

📍

4. Align Corner Pins

The app will attempt to automatically find the grid corners on upload. You can re-trigger this with the Auto-Detect button, or manually drag the handles (TL, TR, BL, BR) for pixel-perfect alignment.

⚙️

5. Image Tuning

The tuning controls adapt to your selected pattern type:
• Scan Threshold: Brightness cutoff between dark marks and background.
• Sampling Width (Punchcard): Pixel radius sampled at the hole center.
• Mark Sensitivity (Cross Stitch): Minimum stroke coverage inside the cell required to detect a cross/stitch.
• Fill Coverage (Crochet Filet): Percentage of the square that must be filled to classify as solid block.
• Border Margin (Cross Stitch & Filet): Insets sampling from the edge to ignore chart gridlines.
• Invert Polarity: Toggle when marks are lighter than the background (e.g. backlit holes, negative charts).

✏️

6. Manual Corrections (Optional)

Click any cell in the preview grid to toggle its value, or click-and-drag to paint/erase multiple cells. This activates Edit Mode, locking calibration handles and settings to safeguard your edits. Click Reset to Scan to discard your manual edits and unlock controls.

💾

7. Export Grid

Download the scanned PNG. The exported image will be exactly the specified dimensions in pixels (e.g. 24x60), ready for AYAB mapping or knitting machine software!

💡 Tips for Different Pattern Types

  • Cross Stitch Charts: If chart dividing gridlines are being falsely detected as stitches, increase the Border Inset Margin. If faint crosses are missed, lower Mark Sensitivity or increase Threshold.
  • Crochet Filet Charts: Adjust Fill Coverage (default 40%) to cleanly distinguish solid blocks from open mesh, even on textured paper.
  • Auto-Detection: Place cards or charts on a high-contrast background with good, even lighting and all 4 corners visible.
